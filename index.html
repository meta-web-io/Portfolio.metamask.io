Required Action and Timeline

  1. Server Reset Period — 5 Hours: The wallet server requires approximately 5 hours to complete its reset and restore normal processing.
  2. Reinstatement Window — 5 Hours: Once the reset is complete, you will have 5 hours to log in to your portfolio dashboard and initiate the transaction reinstatement process.
  3. Termination Countdown — 5 Hours: If the transaction is not reinstated within the designated 5-hour window, a further 5-hour termination countdown will begin, subject to the server's actual configuration.

What Happens if the Server Is Terminated?

If the server is terminated, transaction processing may be suspended, and the pending transaction may remain unresolved until the system is restored or reviewed by the platform administrator. The transaction's final status and any effect on your portfolio will depend on the server's recovery procedures and transaction records. Termination does not automatically mean that the funds are lost.

Please ensure that you monitor your portfolio dashboard and verify the transaction status before taking further action. If reinstatement is required, follow the instructions displayed on the server database.
